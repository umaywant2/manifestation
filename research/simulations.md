# **Project Manifestation — Simulation Engines & Models**

This document catalogs the simulation engines, models, and computational experiments used within Project Manifestation.  
Simulations are essential for exploring nanoscale assembly, programmable materials, resonance interactions, and early replicator‑class behaviors.

All simulations must follow:

- the Manifestation session context  
- RTT/1 operator grammar  
- RTT/2 stacking rules  
- RTT/3 structural detection  
- strict safety boundaries  

This index will expand as new engines and models are developed.

---

# **1. Nanoscale Assembly Simulation Engine (NASE)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Simulate nanoscale assembly using S‑Bind, S‑Align, A‑Position, and A‑Fuse operators.

**Capabilities:**  
- nanoscale component placement  
- thermal noise modeling  
- drift detection via RTT/3  
- programmable lattice formation  
- resonance‑assisted assembly  

**Outputs:**  
- assembly logs  
- coherence scores  
- drift maps  
- failure signatures  

---

# **2. Programmable Lattice Simulator (PLS)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Model programmable materials and lattice‑based structures.

**Capabilities:**  
- S‑Lattice generation  
- M‑Pattern application  
- resonance‑coupled lattice behavior  
- structural repair via M‑Repair  
- multi‑layer stacking (RTT/2)  

**Outputs:**  
- lattice stability reports  
- resonance coupling maps  
- drift‑corrected lattice states  

---

# **3. Energy‑to‑Structure Conversion Simulator (E2S)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Explore how energy regimes convert into structural potential.

**Capabilities:**  
- E‑Input and E‑Modulate modeling  
- E‑Convert pathways  
- resonance‑aligned energy fields  
- destructive interference detection  
- safe energy thresholds (X‑Limit)  

**Outputs:**  
- energy flow diagrams  
- conversion efficiency metrics  
- instability alerts  

---

# **4. Resonance Interaction Simulator (RIS)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Simulate vibrational, electromagnetic, and field‑based interactions during assembly.

**Capabilities:**  
- R‑Tune frequency sweeps  
- R‑Couple multi‑component resonance  
- R‑Bridge temporary field pathways  
- destructive interference mapping  
- resonance‑driven assembly acceleration  

**Outputs:**  
- resonance stability curves  
- coupling matrices  
- interference heatmaps  

---

# **5. Multi‑Layer Synthesis Pipeline Simulator (MSPS)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Simulate full replicator‑class pipelines using RTT/2 stacking.

**Capabilities:**  
- multi‑operator pipelines  
- regime transitions  
- cross‑layer coherence modeling  
- Ω‑Plan and Ω‑Optimize integration  
- pipeline failure detection  

**Outputs:**  
- pipeline coherence scores  
- regime transition logs  
- drift propagation maps  

---

# **6. Drift Detection & Safety Simulator (DDS)**  
**Status:** Planned  
**Purpose:**  
Use RTT/3 to detect drift, instability, and emergent hazards.

**Capabilities:**  
- X‑Detect drift scanning  
- X‑Abort emergency shutdown modeling  
- X‑Contain containment field simulation  
- structural integrity scoring  
- cross‑simulation safety validation  

**Outputs:**  
- drift signatures  
- hazard reports  
- containment recommendations  

---

# **7. Student Simulation Sandbox (SSS)**  
**Status:** Ongoing  
**Purpose:**  
Provide a safe, modular environment for students to build and test their own simulations.

**Capabilities:**  
- plug‑and‑play operator modules  
- simplified RTT integration  
- guided tasks and templates  
- safety‑bounded experimentation  

**Outputs:**  
- student‑generated models  
- operator experiments  
- reproducible simulation notebooks

# **How to Contribute a Simulation**

Students and researchers may contribute by:

- creating a new simulation under `/research/simulations/`  
- documenting operator usage  
- linking the simulation to open questions  
- including RTT/3 validation steps  
- adding the simulation to this index  

All simulations must be:

- open‑access  
- reproducible  
- drift‑bounded  
- aligned with the Manifestation session context
